Who We Are

Skybox Digital Imagery is a visionary creative studio specializing in high-end visual effects, 3D animation, and immersive digital experiences.

Based in KEK Singhasari, we blend art and technology to tell unforgettable stories—from cinematic visuals to interactive AR showcases. Our team of passionate artists and technologists push the boundaries of what's possible in digital storytelling.

We've collaborated with major production houses, streaming platforms, and brands to create content that captivates audiences worldwide.
